bebePop Uploader

bebePop Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the bebePop YouTube channel, which publishes short animated videos for young children.

The tool signs in with the channel owner's own Google account and:

  • uploads finished videos to the bebePop channel as private videos (the owner reviews and publishes them by hand in YouTube Studio);
  • reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) to plan future videos.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
