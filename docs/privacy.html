Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

bebePop Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the bebePop YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload the owner's videos to their own channel as private videos;
  • youtube.readonly – to read the owner's own channel and video information;
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read the owner's own channel statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to review the channel's performance.
  • The sign-in token and any statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect any information about viewers or other YouTube users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the bebePop YouTube channel.
